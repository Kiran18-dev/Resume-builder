You Are Not Alone

Every year 1.5 crore Indians graduate and face the same struggle. No experience, no calls, no hope. But thousands break through every month. The difference? Strategy, not luck.

"Your first job is the hardest. Every job after that gets easier."

📄 Resume Tips for Freshers

1

Keep it to 1 Page

As a fresher your resume should NEVER be more than 1 page. Recruiters spend 6 seconds on a resume. Make every line count.

2

Focus on Projects, Not Marks

No one cares about your CGPA as much as what you've built. Add every project you've worked on — college, personal, or open source.

3

Use Action Verbs

Instead of "I worked on a website", write "Developed a full-stack web application serving 500+ users". Numbers and action verbs get attention.

4

ATS-Friendly Format

Most companies use ATS software to filter resumes. Use simple formatting, no tables, and include keywords from the job description.

5

Add GitHub and LinkedIn

A GitHub profile with active projects and a professional LinkedIn can get you hired even without experience. Set them up today.

🔍 Job Search Strategy

1

Apply to 20 Jobs Per Day

Most freshers apply to 5 jobs and wait. Apply to 20 every single day. It's a numbers game. The more you apply, the faster you get hired.

2

Use LinkedIn, Not Just Naukri

LinkedIn has jobs that are never posted on Naukri. Connect with HR managers directly and message them professionally.

3

Target Startups First

MNCs get 10,000 applications. Startups get 100. Your chances are 100x better. Many startups in India are paying well and growing fast.

4

Use Referrals

65% of jobs are filled through referrals. Message seniors from your college who are already working. Ask for a referral politely.

🔥 Stay Motivated

1

Rejection is Normal

Getting rejected 50-100 times before your first job is completely normal. It's not about your ability. It's about finding the right fit. Keep going.

2

Use the Time to Build Skills

While job hunting, spend 2 hours daily learning. Free courses on YouTube, Coursera and freeCodeCamp can transform your skillset in 3 months.

3

Build in Public

Post your projects on LinkedIn. Share what you're learning. This attracts recruiters and shows initiative even without formal experience.

4

Set a Daily Routine

Treat job hunting like a job. Wake up, apply to jobs, learn skills, build projects, network. Structure prevents despair.
